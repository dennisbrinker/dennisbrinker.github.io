3 Next-Gen Compute Engines

All three compute engines—the CPU, GPU, and NPU—have been rearchitected for intelligent performance, power efficiency, and immersive visual experiences.

CPU: Redesigned Cores for Speed and Efficiency

In addition to new P-cores and E-cores, select Intel® Core™ Ultra processors (Series 3) come with new low-power E-cores for long battery life.

GPU: New Architecture for Enhanced Immersion

The built-in Intel® Arc™ GPU in select SKUs redefines graphics and AI capabilities, with the new X3 graphics architecture enabling up to 12 Xe cores and 120 GPU TOPS.

NPU: Delivering Powerful AI at Scale

Every Intel® Core™ Ultra processor (Series 3) comes with a rearchitected NPU that supports Copilot+, delivers up to 50 TOPS, and offers fast and efficient processing for generative AI workloads.

3 Platform Advances

The new processor platform enhances connections and collaboration for on-the-go customers

Premium Connectivity

Intel® Wi-Fi 7 R2 (5 Gig) and dual-channel Bluetooth® 6 deliver better connection stability and faster data transfer speeds, and discrete support for Thunderbolt™ 5 ports streamlines multi-device connectivity and file sharing.

Higher-Quality Video Collaboration

The new image processing unit, IPU 7.5, redefines video conferencing, providing an exceptional experience in any lighting conditions. IPU 7.5 supports up to three high-resolution webcams while also reducing power draw for longer battery life.1

More Daisy-Chaining

The latest Xe display engine allows customers to connect to up to four 6K screens for video editing, visual engineering, and other tasks that require a large, high-resolution workspace.

3 Types of SKUs

As shown in the table below, there are three types of Intel® Core™ Ultra mobile processor (Series 3) SKUs:

Intel® Core™ Ultra processor X9 388H

For maximum graphics capability, X7 and X9 SKUs come with an Intel® Arc™ GPU that features 12 Xe cores and up to an astonishing 122 GPU TOPS.

Intel® Core™ Ultra 9 processor 386H

The “H” stands for “high-performance,” and the latest H SKUs have 16 total cores, all of them rearchitected for maximum performance and power efficiency.

Intel® Core™ Ultra 5 processor 325

Intel® Core™ Ultra 5 and 7 processors are available with no suffix. With six or eight total cores, they provide a premium AI PC option at a lower price point.
